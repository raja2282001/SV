## Brand & Style

This design system serves a premier commercial printing, photocopying, and institutional stationery hub situated in Ahmedabad, Gujarat. Rooted in utility, accuracy, and operational reliability, the system evokes the atmosphere of an engineered print studio: crisp registration marks, tactile paper stocks, and razor-sharp typographic clarity. 

The target audience encompasses architectural and engineering firms requiring large-format blueprints, university students needing thesis binding, corporate offices procuring quarterly stationery, and local retail consumers seeking rapid, high-fidelity reprographic services.

The design movement combines **Modern Utilitarian Editorial** with **Industrial Precision**. Rather than emulating transactional e-commerce platforms, the interface functions strictly as a structured digital catalogue and specification manual. Interactivity centers around product exploration, service capability reviews, custom print job spec-building, and immediate offline or direct communication routes (WhatsApp direct messaging, phone consultation, and shop visits). Visual weight relies on balanced color blocking, ultra-crisp borders, and disciplined layout rhythm instead of transactional visual clutter.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative industrial tones with active, eye-catching visual accents:

- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Deep ink slate/navy. Serves as the bedrock color for high-contrast typography, structural headers, high-importance badges, and grounding frames. Evokes toner density and professional authority.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`)**: Warm industrial amber/yellow. Used for primary inquiry highlights, key capability tags, highlight rules, and energetic focal points. Pairs against deep navy to evoke classic reprographic shop signage.
- **Tertiary (`#2563EB`)**: Electric technical blue. Reserved for technical specifications, active blueprint/CAD indicators, service tags, and secondary action triggers.
- **Accent Alert / Bulk Price (`#EA580C`)**: Deep vermilion amber used sparingly for volume-tier price discounts, urgent stock notifications, and rapid-turnaround badges.
- **Surface & Canvas (`#FFFFFF` and `#F8FAFC`)**: Ultra-clean presentation plane with alternating panels in `#F1F5F9` to subtly differentiate technical specifications from product grids.
- **Structural Borders (`#E2E8F0`)**: Hard, distinct boundary lines delivering a blueprint-like grid feel across all cards and containers.

## Typography

The type system blends the geometric, architectural precision of **Space Grotesk** with the ergonomic legibility of **Plus Jakarta Sans**.

- **Display & Headings (Space Grotesk)**: Conveys technical authority, machine-calibrated precision, and industrial print lineage. Used across hero headers, product model names, service category titles, and pricing metrics.
- **Body & Specifications (Plus Jakarta Sans)**: Delivers rapid scannability for paper weights (GSM), finish varieties (matte, gloss, soft-touch), dimensions (A4, A3, A0, jumbo), and service instructions.
- **Labels & Numbers (Space Grotesk)**: Enforces crisp visual hierarchy for catalog numbers, status badges, and Indian Rupee (`₹`) pricing notations. Rupee currency figures must always be formatted with tabular clarity using medium or semi-bold weights (e.g., `₹2.50 / page` or `₹450 / ream`).

## Layout & Spacing

The catalogue is built on a strictly calibrated 12-column grid on desktop, collapsing to 6 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Desktop (min-width: 1200px)**: 12 columns, `1.5rem` gutters, `3rem` screen margins. Layout maximum width capped at `1440px` centered to maintain technical scannability without wide line strain.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 6 columns, `1.25rem` gutters, `2rem` screen margins. Secondary sidebars collapse into sticky bottom or top drawer filters.
- **Mobile (under 768px)**: 4 columns, `1rem` gutters, `1.25rem` screen margins. Product and service cards transition into single-column vertical stacks or dual-column metric grids.

Vertical rhythm adheres strictly to an 8px base grid system (`space-xs` = 4px, `space-sm` = 8px, `space-md` = 16px, `space-lg` = 24px, `space-xl` = 40px). Information density is prioritized: cards utilize compact inner padding to present comprehensive specifications (GSM, Dimensions, Binding Options, Finish, Pack Size) without excessive vertical pagination.

## Elevation & Depth

To reflect physical paper handling and technical drafting sheets, this design system rejects heavy, blurry drop shadows in favor of **Crisp Low-Contrast Outlines** combined with **Engineered Tonal Surfaces**:

- **Ground Level (Canvas)**: `#FFFFFF` or `#F8FAFC`.
- **Card & Component Planes**: `#FFFFFF` overlaid with a continuous 1px solid border in `#E2E8F0`.
- **Hover / Focus Lift**: Rather than large diffused elevation, elements transition with a crisp border shift to `#0F172A` (or `#2563EB`) accompanied by a precise 2px physical offset shadow: `box-shadow: 3px 3px 0px 0px #0F172A`. This introduces a tactile, printed-die effect.
- **Drawers & Floating Modals (Inquiry Sheets)**: Tinted backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`) backed by `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.4)` and framed with 1px solid `#CBD5E1`.
- **Badge & Chip Elevation**: Flat surface with 1px border. No shadows.

## Shapes

The design system maintains a **Soft-Industrial** structural geometry with a `roundedness` level of `1`. 

- **Standard Cards & Containers**: `4px` (`0.25rem`) border-radius. Evokes square-cut paper stacks, guillotine-trimmed stationery, and blueprint folios.
- **Interactive Badges, Action Buttons, and Inputs**: `4px` (`0.25rem`) to `6px` radius. Clean, disciplined, and purposeful.
- **Category Chips**: `4px` (`0.25rem`) border-radius for an organized index-card aesthetic.
- **Strict Prohibition**: Circular pill shapes (`9999px`) are forbidden across standard actions to prevent an overly playful consumer e-commerce feel.

## Components

### 1. Buttons (Catalogue & Inquiry Exclusive)
- **Prohibition**: No "Add to Cart", "Buy Now", or checkout buttons exist anywhere in the interface.
- **Primary Action ("Inquire on WhatsApp" / "Request Quote")**: Deep Navy background (`#0F172A`), white text, Space Grotesk label font, 1px solid `#0F172A`. On hover, swaps to Warm Amber (`#F59E0B`) background with Navy text and a sharp 2px tactile offset.
- **Secondary Action ("Call Store" / "Download Spec Sheet")**: White background, Navy text, 1px solid `#CBD5E1`. On hover, background shifts to `#F1F5F9`.
- **Direct Visit Action ("View Ahmedabad Store Location")**: Subtle Electric Blue tint (`#EFF6FF`), Blue text (`#2563EB`), 1px solid `#BFDBFE`.

### 2. Product & Stationery Cards
- **Architecture**: Contained within 1px solid `#E2E8F0` on white, transitioning to `#F8FAFC` for the spec drawer section of the card.
- **Visual Section**: Crisp product photo or paper texture sample on neutral `#F1F5F9` background with an aspect ratio of 4:3.
- **Header Section**: Upper badge cluster showing category (e.g., "75 GSM Copier Paper", "Spiral Binding", "Architectural CAD Plotting") and availability ("In Store Stock", "Bulk Order Only").
- **Pricing Notation**: Displayed in prominent Space Grotesk: `₹ [Amount]` with unit qualification (`/ ream`, `/ sq.ft`, `/ copy`, or `Rate on Request`).
- **Footer Section**: Direct "Inquire Specifications" CTA button passing pre-filled product codes to communication channels.

### 3. Service Showcase Blocks (Xerox & Large-Format Printing)
- Grid cards dedicated to high-volume capabilities: B&W High-Speed Xerox, Digital Color Production, Thesis Hard Binding, Lamination (Gloss/Matte), Plotter Blueprint Printing, and Scan-to-Drive.
- Metric-driven spec strip showing: "Max Width: 36 inches", "Turnaround: Same Day", "Paper Types: 70-350 GSM".

### 4. Badge Tags & Chips
- **Category Badges**: All-caps micro-type (`Space Grotesk`, `label-sm`), background `#F1F5F9`, border `#E2E8F0`, text `#1E293B`.
- **Status: In Stock**: Background `#ECFDF5`, border `#A7F3D0`, text `#065F46`.
- **Status: Fast Delivery / Ahmedabad Local**: Background `#FFFBEB`, border `#FDE68A`, text `#92400E`.
- **Special Tier / Discount**: Background `#FFF7ED`, border `#FED7AA`, text `#C2410C`.

### 5. Input Fields & Search / Filter Controls
- **Search Bar**: Wide layout equipped with Space Grotesk prompt ("Search by GSM, paper brand, binding type, or service..."), 1px solid `#CBD5E1`, inset left icon, focusing to `#0F172A` with an energetic yellow indicator ring.
- **Filter Chips**: Multi-select toggle buttons with clean checkmark states for fast filtering (e.g., "JK Copier", "A4", "A3", "100+ GSM", "Lamination").

### 6. Shop Visit & Direct Inquiry Action Bar
- Sticky mobile footer or prominent desktop right-hand panel displaying current store open/closed status in Ahmedabad (IST), full postal address, direct phone contact, and instant WhatsApp inquiry routing.
